<style_guide language="en" applies_to="translations into English">
Write what a native-speaking engineer would write, and do not trace Vietnamese word order. Keep the register: casual stays casual and formal stays formal. Technical terms the source already wrote in English stay as written.

<example>
Vietnamese: Mình deploy xong rồi nhưng cache lỗi nên latency tăng gấp đôi.
Traced: I deployed finished already but the cache has an error so the latency increases double.
Written: I've finished the deploy, but a cache bug doubled our latency.
</example>
</style_guide>
