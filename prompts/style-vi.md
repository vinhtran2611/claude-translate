Voice: two engineers talking, relaxed and direct, never textbook. Keep the author's tone, humor and emphasis.

Write Vietnamese, not English traced word by word. Work out what the sentence means, then say it the way a Vietnamese speaker would: reorder clauses when Vietnamese wants it, split long sentences, drop empty fillers such as "simply" or "actually".

Terminology: for each technical word, ask which language a Vietnamese engineer would use for it in a standup. Keep English when they would say English (tools, concepts, products, acronyms, metrics); write Vietnamese when they would say Vietnamese (everyday words). Use one form per term, because a reader who sees two forms assumes two meanings, and do not add the English in parentheses after a Vietnamese word. The glossary settles recurring cases and overrides your judgment.
