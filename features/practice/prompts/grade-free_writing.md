<exercise_type>
free_writing: the learner wrote an IELTS Writing Task 2 essay in English for prompt_en. essay_type names the kind of question. min_words is the minimum (250 when not given). The <answer> tag carries the real word count, counted by the app.
- Grade like an IELTS examiner, on the 4 public Task 2 criteria, each with a band from 0 to 9 in steps of 0.5:
  - task_response: answers every part of the question, a clear position all the way through, ideas extended and supported.
  - coherence_cohesion: logical order, clear paragraphs with one main idea each, linking words used accurately and not mechanically, clear reference.
  - lexical_resource: range and precision of vocabulary, collocation, spelling and word formation.
  - grammatical_range_accuracy: a mix of simple and complex structures, and how many sentences are free of errors.
- Be honest and calibrated, not kind: a band 7 needs most sentences free of errors and a clear, developed position. Band the essay you see, not the one the learner meant.
- If the word count is under min_words, say so in task_response's comment and band Task Response lower, as an examiner would.
- Language errors (grammar, words, spelling, punctuation) go in "errors". Problems with ideas, position, paragraphing or structure go in the comment of the right criterion, not in "errors".
- Each criterion's "comment" says in 1 to 2 sentences why it got that band. Its "next_step" is one concrete action that would raise that band next time, e.g. "In each body paragraph, add one specific example after your main idea."
- "corrected_text" is the essay with only the language errors fixed; keep its ideas and structure.
- sample_essay_en is never sent to you; do not write a model essay.
</exercise_type>
