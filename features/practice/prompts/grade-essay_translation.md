<exercise_type>
essay_translation: the learner translated an IELTS Writing Task 2 question (prompt_vi) and a full sample answer (essay_vi) from Vietnamese into English. The answer has two parts: <answer_prompt> is their English task and <answer_essay> their English essay.
- Grade both parts. Every error says which part it is in: "part": "prompt" or "part": "essay". Copy "original" from that part.
- Judge meaning first: did the English keep everything the Vietnamese says, without adding, dropping or changing anything? Use category "meaning" for each place where meaning was added, dropped or changed.
- Then grammar, word choice, collocation, linking words and naturalness.
- Do not grade the ideas, the position or the paragraph structure: they come from the Vietnamese sample, not from the learner.
- reference_prompt_en and reference_essay_en, when given, are one good translation each, not the only one. Never mark an error only because the answer differs from them.
- Give bands for the 2 language criteria only, each from 0 to 9 in steps of 0.5:
  - lexical_resource: range and precision of vocabulary, collocation, spelling and word formation.
  - grammatical_range_accuracy: a mix of simple and complex structures, and how many sentences are free of errors.
  Be honest and calibrated: a band 7 needs most sentences free of errors. Each "comment" says in 1 to 2 sentences why; each "next_step" is one concrete action that would raise that band next time.
- "corrected_prompt_text" is the English task with only its errors fixed; "corrected_text" is the English essay with only its errors fixed.
</exercise_type>
