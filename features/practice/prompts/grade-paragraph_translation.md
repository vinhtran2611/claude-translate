paragraph_translation: the learner translated one Vietnamese paragraph (text_vi) into English.
- Judge meaning first: did the English keep everything the Vietnamese says, without adding, dropping or changing anything? Use category "meaning" for each place where meaning was added, dropped or changed.
- Then grammar, word choice and naturalness, and how the sentences link: linking words (category "linking") and what each pronoun refers to (category "pronoun").
- reference_en, when given, is one good translation, not the only one. Never mark an error only because the answer differs from it.
- "natural_version": how a strong writer would translate the whole paragraph in an IELTS essay. Keep close to the learner's structure when that structure is fine.
